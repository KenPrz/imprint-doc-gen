PDF operations

Merge PDFs, select pages, apply text watermarks and numbering, and attach supporting files.

Merge and select pages

POST /v1/pdf
{
  "inputs":[
    {"bytes":"BASE64_FIRST_PDF", "pages":[1,3]},
    {"bytes":"BASE64_SECOND_PDF"}
  ],
  "watermark":{"text":"DRAFT", "opacity":0.2, "rotation":30},
  "pageNumbers":true,
  "attachments":[{"name":"details.csv", "bytes":"BASE64_CSV", "mimeType":"text/csv"}]
}

Page indexes are one-based. Omitting pages selects all pages in that input. Inputs and selected indexes define output order; duplicates are allowed. The response is binary PDF. Page numbers appear at the bottom center.

Split into groups

POST /v1/pdf/split
{"bytes":"BASE64_PDF", "ranges":[[1,2],[3],[4,5]]}

The response is {"documents":["BASE64_PDF",...]}. SDK exports processPdf({inputs,...options}) and splitPdf(bytes, ranges) accept byte arrays and return buffers.

Limits and document behavior

  • At most 20 inputs, 1,000 source/output pages and 32 MiB combined input/attachment bytes. Output is also bounded at 32 MiB.
  • At most 20 attachments with unique simple filenames. At most 20 split groups and 1,000 selected pages across groups.
  • HTTP operations run in a 256 MiB worker with a 30-second deadline and shared admission capacity.
  • Watermarks use built-in Helvetica and its supported Latin character set. Unsupported characters fail explicitly.
  • Encrypted, signed, interactive-form and detected active-content PDFs are rejected. This is document assembly, not a PDF sanitization or malware-scanning service.
  • Assembly copies pages into a new document. Document-level bookmarks, original attachments, metadata and cross-document navigation are not guaranteed to survive. Supply attachments explicitly.
  • No PDF/A, PDF/UA, digital signature or redaction conformance is claimed.

Low-level PDF manipulation uses pdf-lib behind Imprint's validation and execution limits.
